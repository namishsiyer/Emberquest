📖 Adventurer's Guide (How to Play)

⚡ How Experience (XP) & Levels Work

Every time you log a productive real-life habit, you earn XP. High-priority habits (like Studies & Workouts) give a 1.35x XP bonus! As XP fills your meter, you level up, unlocking higher ranks from Rookie to Legend.

🔥 Priorities & Habit Multipliers

Tag habits as High (🔥), Medium (⚡), or Low (🌱). Click any activity card to inspect last 7 days of logs, view trend percentages, and change priority.

🧬 Real-Time Morphology

Your pixel hero transforms based on your daily actions:

  • Workouts & Running: Increases Muscle Tone, sculpts abdominal lines, and broadens shoulders (Toned, Athletic, Shredded).
  • Skipping Days: Muscles gradually shrink into a Soft or Frail build.
  • Junk Food: Adds body fat and expands the hero's belly (Heavy).
👑 Gear Unlocks

As you level up, gear equips automatically onto your sprite: Sweatband at Lv2, Fire Sneakers at Lv3, Wristbands at Lv5, Hero Cape at Lv7, Flame Aura at Lv10, and Golden Crown at Lv15!

🛡️ Daily Decay & Shields

If you don't log any activities during a day, your score suffers from Inactivity Decay and streaks drop. However, each level-up grants a Shield (🛡️) (max 3) which automatically sacrifices itself to save your XP and streak!

🔄 Penalty Refund

Forgot to log yesterday? Just backfill your activity, and the system automatically restores your lost XP or refund your shield!

👾 Weekly Nemesis Boss Raids

Every week, a monstrous boss (like Procrastination Slime or Couch Golem) attacks! Every point of XP you earn deals direct damage to the boss's HP. Defeating the boss grants massive bonus XP.

📜 Daily Quests & Radar Balance

Complete at least 5 of 7 daily quests to trigger the Daily Sweep +50 XP bonus. Keep an eye on your 7-axis glowing Radar Chart to maintain equilibrium across Strength, Stamina, Focus, Knowledge, Recovery, Fuel, and Reflection.
